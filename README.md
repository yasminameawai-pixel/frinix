
FriNiX

Vision and Main Goal
This is an AI-powered smartwatch specifically designed to monitor and detect psychological stress in children in real time, enabling parents to understand their child's unspoken emotional state and intervene early to support them.

Problem Addressed by the Project
The watch targets children experiencing situations or circumstances that cause them severe psychological or emotional stress, which they are unable to express or suppress, thus impacting their mental and physical well-being without their parents' knowledge.
